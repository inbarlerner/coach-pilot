# Card: Carve turns, twin tip (קארב: החלפת כיוון) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Toe to Heel Carve, Heel to Toe Carve). Duotone "BEGINNER Jibe heel to toe / toe to heel" still to pull (YouTube block).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.carve` |
| Level | Beginner → intermediate |
| Prerequisites | Comfortable toeside (see [toeside.md](toeside.md)) · one-hand kite control |
| What it is | Changing direction by carving the board through a half-circle on an edge while the kite crosses the window. Two variants: **A. heel → toe** and **B. toe → heel**. |
| Common rule | **Kite first, then board**, with no pause between. The kite crosses *below* 12. The speed of the kite and the radius of the carve must match. |

## A. Heel → toe carve

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Approach | Gentle heelside edge, lines tight, kite no higher than 11/1, hands centred. Check the space. | Normal heelside position, lines ~45° forward. |
| A2 | Kite first | Steer the kite positively with both hands (push and pull), turning it to face back across the window. | Bar clearly tilted; kite starting to cross. |
| A3 | Flatten | Rock over the board as the hands push forward; board goes flat. | Board flat, rider centred over it. |
| A4 | Carve onto the toes | Flex the ankles, drive the knees forward onto the toe edge, weight slightly back over the tail, head up, upright body. | Board banked on the toe edge, turning downwind; rider upright, not bent at the waist. |
| A5 | Control the kite | Halfway (board pointing downwind): level the bar so the kite flies straight; small adjustments. | Rider steady, board pointing downwind, kite moving across low. |
| A6 | Finish | Keep carving back upwind on the toe edge; find tension by edging, not by pulling the bar in. | Toeside riding position on the new tack. |

## B. Toe → heel carve

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Approach | Solid toeside edge, kite no higher than 1/11. | Toeside position, lines ~45° forward. |
| B2 | Kite first | Front hand off; steer the kite with the back hand (near the centre line) using the wrist, cutting a little off the top of the window. | One hand on the bar; kite starting to cross. |
| B3 | Hips back | Move only the hips back over the tail; shoulders stay upright. Stay on the edge until the kite starts to pull the other way. | Hips behind the back foot, nose lifting, upper body upright. |
| B4 | Flatten and roll | Flatten the board, then compress onto the back leg and roll onto the heels. Head turns to look where you're going. | Board turning on the heel edge; upper body upright, lower body loaded. |
| B5 | Free hand back on | Reach up with the free hand and grab the bar; this brings the weight forward and puts more edge in the water. | Both hands reaching the bar; board on the heel edge, coming around. |
| B6 | Finish | Release the new front hand, twist upwind, back into the heelside stance. | Heelside riding position on the new tack, kite under control. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Kite goes over 12 | Lines vertical mid-turn; rider stops under the kite and sinks. | Steer more decisively and keep the kite low. |
| Kite finishes before the rider | Kite already on the new side while the board is still pointing downwind; rider pulled off balance. | Slow the kite or carve faster; match the two. |
| Bent at the waist | Rider bending over during the carve; bum upwind of the edge; carve stops. | Head and body up, drive with ankles and knees. |
| Pulling the bar in to steer / for power | Bar at the chest; kite stalls mid-turn. | Steer with the wrist; find power by edging. |
| Whole body leans back (toe → heel) | Rider falls onto the bum or back as the kite pulls. | Only the hips go back; shoulders stay up. |
| Board edging too long | Kite lifts the rider off the edge. | Flatten as soon as the kite pulls the other way. |

## Coaching decisions applied
- "Head leads" ([coaching-decisions.md](../../coaching-decisions.md)) applies here as: look where you're going through the carve, and the body follows.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- A carve takes ~3–5 s. Spread the frames evenly across the turn.
