# Card: Riding upwind (עלייה לרוח) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from Advakite (Upwind), Get High with Mike (riding slowly upwind) and IKSURFMAG (Stance).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.upwind` |
| Level | Beginner → lifelong |
| Prerequisites | Waterstart, riding both directions |
| What it is | Turning the kite's pull into progress against the wind: lean back on the lines, edge the board, ride at the right speed. |

## The three elements

| # | Element | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lean on the lines | Hang back in the harness against the kite with a firm, straight-ish body, not upright over the board. | Body leaning well back against the lines (~45°); hips up; rider clearly "hanging" on the lines. |
| 2 | Edge the board | Board on its heel edge at roughly the same angle as the lean. Use the whole edge to resist the pull, not just the fins. | Board tilted on its heel rail, spray from the rail along the board's length. |
| 3 | Right speed | Just fast enough to plane. Riding fast pushes the kite deeper in the window and takes you downwind. | Moderate spray, not a rooster tail; kite toward the edge of the window (lines well forward). |

Kite position: around 45° (10–11 or 1–2), bar eased out to the sweet spot so the kite flies to the edge of the window.

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Standing upright | Body vertical over the board; board flat; rider drifting downwind. | Lean back and hang on the lines; edge comes with the lean. |
| Riding too fast | Big spray, high speed, kite deep in the window (lines less forward); losing ground. | Slow down: lean and edge harder, bar out to the sweet spot. |
| Sitting down | Bum near the water; board flat; bouncing. | Hips up, legs push the board away. |
| Pulling the bar in | Bar at the chest; kite deep in the window, pulling downwind. | Bar out to the sweet spot so the kite flies forward. |
| Kite too high | Lines near vertical; rider lifted off the edge. | Kite lower (~45°). |
| Flat board steering with the tail | Board flat, rider pivoting on the fins; slides out when power increases. | Use the whole edge; lean and edge together. |

## ⚠️ For Inbar: the sources disagree
**How much to twist toward upwind, and where the weight goes.**
- **Advakite:** focusing on twisting the shoulders and looking upwind is "the worst advice": riders stay upright and lose the lean. Lean back first; avoid shifting to the tail.
- **Mike:** twist aggressively upwind, take the front hand off the bar to open the chest, nearly straighten the front leg, bend the back leg, weight on the back of the board as a rudder, ride slowly.
- **IKSURFMAG:** shoulders lean back *and* twist upwind; look further upwind than seems possible; rock weight forward when underpowered, back when powered.

**Draft position:** lean first (all agree it's the most important), then twist upwind. **Inbar should decide** whether to teach the weight-back "rudder" version.

## Drills
- On the beach with the kite at 12: lean back slowly on the lines to feel the range of angles, then remember a medium lean.
- On the water: end every run by increasing the lean and keeping the body firm.
- Compare two runs, fast and slow; the slow one usually gains more ground.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- A few seconds of steady riding is enough. The best view is side-on, showing the lean angle and the board's edge.
