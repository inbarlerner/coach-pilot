Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Coachit is a pilot platform where trainees upload sports videos and coaches send back an analysis. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Coachit is run by its founding team. For any privacy question or request, email coachitapp@gmail.com.

Who can use Coachit

Coachit is for people 18 or older. When you sign up you confirm that you are 18 or older. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: first and last name, username, email address, phone number, the languages you speak and the sports you are interested in. Coaches also give a short professional bio and the sports they coach.
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook: we receive your name, email address and profile picture link from that service. We never receive your Google or Facebook password.
  • Profile photo, if you add one.
  • Your content: videos you upload and their cover images, the questions you send with them, comments, and reports about content.
  • Coaching content: coaches' analyses (text, voice recordings, video recordings and drawings on the video) and the follow-up messages between a trainee and a coach.
  • Technical data: your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and operation.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the service: create your account, send your video to the coach you choose, deliver the analysis and messages, and show you notifications.
  • To show public profiles and public videos to other users.
  • To keep the platform safe: approving coaches, handling reports and removing content that breaks the rules.
  • To build and test our AI coach. See AI analysis below.
  • To contact you about your account.

Who can see what

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, languages, sports and, for coaches, bio): other signed-in users. Profile photos are stored at a public link.
  • Your email and phone number: only you and the Coachit admins.
  • Public videos and their comments: other signed-in users. Completed analyses of a public video can be shown with it.
  • Private videos: you, the coaches you send them to, and the Coachit admins.
  • Requests and analyses: the trainee and the coach involved, and the Coachit admins.
  • Follow-up messages: only the trainee and the coach involved.

Private files are only ever handed out through links that expire after one hour.

Services we use

We use these providers to run Coachit. They process data on our behalf, and their servers may be outside Israel.

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting.
  • Google and Meta (Facebook): only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Anthropic: AI analysis (see below).

AI analysis

We are testing an AI coach. To do that, the Coachit admins may run it on videos uploaded to Coachit. For each test, still frames from the video, the sport and the trainee's question are sent to Anthropic, whose AI model writes a draft analysis. The admins review these drafts to improve the AI coach. We keep the frames and the draft for that review. The full video file is not sent.

How long we keep data

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • The pilot has limited storage. When it fills up, the oldest video files and coach recordings are removed automatically, and the owner is notified. The request, the analysis text and the messages are kept. Videos in an open request are never removed.
  • When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: sign in, open Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. By email: write to coachitapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, email, phone number, profile photo, videos, comments and your requests with coaches, including the coaches' analyses of your videos.

If you are a coach: your open requests are cancelled and the trainees are told. Trainees keep the written analyses, drawings and messages you already sent them, shown as from a "Former coach", and follow-up messages with you are closed. Your voice and video recordings are deleted.

Admin accounts cannot be deleted in the app; email us instead.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Coachit from your Facebook account under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. If you signed in with Google, you can do the same at myaccount.google.com/connections. This stops the sign-in link but does not delete your Coachit data. Use the steps above for that.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can edit most of your details yourself in Settings. For anything else, email coachitapp@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or, if you live in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Security

Access rules are enforced on our servers, not only in the app: each user can only reach the data they are allowed to see. No method of storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
