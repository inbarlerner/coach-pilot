# Card: Body drag upwind (בודי דראג) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Body Dragging Upwind). Duotone "BEGINNER Bodydrag" still to pull (YouTube block).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.body_drag_upwind` |
| Level | Beginner (safety skill) |
| Prerequisites | Basic kite control: steering with one hand, relaunch |
| What it is | Getting upwind in the water without the board, to recover a lost board or get back to the beach. The body acts as the "edge". |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Locate the board | After a crash: stop, breathe, find the board before dragging. | Rider still in the water, head up, looking around; kite parked high. |
| 2 | Kite to the side | Fly the kite at 10–11:30 or 12:30–2, not straight overhead and not on the water. Back hand holds the bar near the centre line; the bar is pulled only until the lines are tight. | Lines leaning ~30–60° to the side; one hand on the bar near the centre. |
| 3 | Drag position | Roll onto the front, side-on to the pull. Free (leading) arm stretched out toward the target, legs stretched out behind and locked: the body acts as a long edge. | Body horizontal in the water, side-on; leading arm pointing forward and upwind; legs together and straight behind. |
| 4 | Work the kite | Steer the kite down for power (lift the elbow and twist the wrist like opening a bottle), back up gently before it pulls you downwind. | Kite moving in small up-and-down strokes on one side of the window; rider moving forward through the water at an upwind angle. |
| 5 | Turn | Slowly: kite gently up toward 12, lean back with legs kicked out in front, ease the bar out, swap hands overhead, stretch out the new leading arm, then steer the kite down on the new side. | Rider briefly upright, leaning back with feet forward; kite near 12; then the mirror image of stage 3. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Kite too high | Lines near vertical; rider lifted out of the water and dragged straight downwind. | Kite lower to the side (10–11:30 or 12:30–2). |
| Kite too low / on the water | Kite near the water; rider ploughing face-first; kite drops in a lull. | Bring it up a bit; work it up and down. |
| Pulling the bar in for power | Bar pulled to the body; kite stalls and tumbles (light wind) or drifts deep downwind (strong wind). | Bar only until the lines are tight; get power by moving the kite, not by pulling. |
| Body upright instead of side-on | Rider vertical in the water, legs dangling. No "edge", so no upwind progress. | Roll onto the front, leading arm out, legs straight behind. |
| Fast, jerky turns | Kite swung quickly through 12; rider pulled downwind at every turn. | Everything in slow motion during the turn. |
| Lost track of the board | Long runs with the face in the water, no stops to check. | Short runs, and look for the board during each turn (best view). |

## Drills
- Practise in deep, safe water with a friend holding your board: short runs on both sides.
- In light wind, kick with the legs and claw with the free arm.
- Dragging back in with the board: hold it out in front, under the front arm, between you and the kite.

## Safety
Always know where the board is. If the board is lost in waves or toward obstacles, prioritise your own safety over the board.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- A body drag is long and slow: spread the frames evenly across the clip (no takeoff tap needed).
- The rider is low in the water and often small in frame: say so if the body position isn't visible.
