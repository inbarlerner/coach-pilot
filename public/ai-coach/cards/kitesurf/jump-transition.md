# Card: Jump transition (ג'אמפ טרנזישן) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Jump Transition), Steven Akkersdijk (Jump Transition: all you need to know; 6 beginner friendly tricks). Duotone "HOOKED Jump transition" still to pull (YouTube block).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.hooked.jump_transition` |
| Level | Beginner → intermediate |
| Prerequisites | [Hooked jump](hooked-jump.md) with a controlled landing · slide/carve turn |
| What it is | A jump that lands going the other way: take off upwind, float with the kite parked just past 12, redirect the kite and land in the new direction. Think of it as a jump you decide to land the other way, not an aerial slide turn. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Solid upwind edge, kite at 11/1, hands about halfway in. **Slow down by edging upwind and easing the bar out**, not by drifting the kite up. | Heelside edge, lines ~45° forward, moderate speed. |
| 2 | Send | Steady send from 11/1 to just past 12. The more power, the gentler the send. | Lines rising to vertical. |
| 3 | Carve + kick | Carve **all the way upwind**, board pointing into the wind. As the kite reaches 12: level the bar, stamp off the water by extending the legs against the edge, **then** pull the bar in. Order: level → stamp → pull. | Board pointing straight upwind at takeoff with heavy spray; lines vertical; legs extending; bar level. |
| 4 | Float | Bar in and level; kite parked at 11:30/12:30. Keep looking **forward** (the jump direction); knees up. | Lines vertical or just past; bar level and in; rider compact, head still facing the original direction. |
| 5 | Head turns, redirect | At or just after the apex: **the head leads the turn** and looks *downwind* toward the landing (not back the way you came). Dive the kite firmly toward the new direction with both hands. | Head turned toward downwind; bar tilted hard; lines swinging toward the new side. |
| 6 | Land + ride out | Legs down; twist the hips so the board points slightly downwind in the new direction; land tail first; carve back to an upwind edge. | Board pointing downwind-ish in the new direction at touchdown; kite diving; spray. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Not carving far enough upwind** (the key point, per SA) | Board crosswind at takeoff; rider keeps travelling sideways in the air; kite flies to the edge of the window, no power to land and ride away. | Carve until the board points into the wind; take off upwind. |
| Too much speed | Rider swings like a pendulum under the kite; lands far downwind with no power. | Slow down with edge and bar out; the stamp also brakes. |
| Kite steered too far past 12 | Lines leaning well back (toward the new side) at the apex; diving it gives no power. | Park at 11:30/12:30; level the bar at 12. |
| Turning the head back too early | Head turned back toward the original upwind direction during the climb; kite drifts across; landing on an edge. | Look forward on the way up; head turns on the way down, toward **downwind**. |
| Gentle dive | Kite drifts down the edge of the window; no pull on landing. | Firm push-pull dive with both hands. |
| Landing on an edge / across the wind | Board perpendicular to the direction of movement; abrupt stop. | Twist the board to point slightly downwind and land tail first. |
| Aerial slide turn instead of a jump | Rider slides the back foot out and hops round low. | Edge all the way to takeoff. |

## Coaching decisions applied
- **Head always leads** ([coaching-decisions.md](../../coaching-decisions.md)): the head starts the turn to the new direction, timed for the descent, and the hips and board follow.

## Drills
- On-water carve drill: ride crosswind at medium to high speed, lean back and carve hard upwind (toes up, heels down, back leg slightly bent, bum low) until almost stopped, then ride on.
- Start with small jump transitions; it's fine to land on your bum at first, then work on the landing.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- From ~3 s before takeoff to ~2 s after landing; densest around the carve/takeoff and the redirect.
- Key checks: board direction at takeoff (upwind?), line angle at the apex, head direction on the way down, board direction at touchdown.

## Progression notes (separate cards)
- **Loop-out jump transition:** the kite loops after the apex. Bar fully in and steer hard for a small loop; follow the power downwind.
- **Back roll jump transition:** see [back-roll-transition.md](back-roll-transition.md).
