# Card: Jump with a grab (קפיצה עם גראב) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Jump with Tweaked Boned Grab) and Steven Akkersdijk (6 Beginner Friendly Tricks). Duotone "HOOKED Jump grab" still to pull (YouTube block).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.hooked.jump_grab` |
| Level | Beginner → intermediate |
| Prerequisites | [Hooked jump](hooked-jump.md) with controlled landings · steering the kite with one hand |
| What it is | A normal jump where the back hand lets go of the bar at the top and grabs the board (tail or heel edge near the back foot); optionally "boned": front leg pushed straight. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hand position | Ride and jump with the front hand **next to the centre line** (power line between the index and middle finger), so the kite doesn't steer when the back hand lets go. | Front hand touching the centre of the bar. |
| 2 | Jump | A normal jump with a good edge and a vertical takeoff (see the hooked jump card). Get off the water **first**; grab second. | Same as the hooked jump: spray, board pointing upwind, lines near vertical. |
| 3 | Rise | Bar in and level, kite parked just behind 12 (11:30/12:30). Knees up toward the bar so the board comes to the hand. | Bar in; knees lifting; board rising in front of the body. |
| 4 | Release + grab | Back hand lets go once the rider is up and the kite is high; reaches the tail or heel edge by the back foot. Grab on the way up or at the top at the latest. Upright body, not leaning back. | One hand on the bar near the centre; back hand on the board; knees tucked; torso upright. |
| 5 | Tweak (optional) | Pull the board in with the grab hand and push the front leg straight ("boned"). | Front leg straight, back leg tucked, board angled across the body. |
| 6 | Release + land | Let go of the board as you start to descend (or at the last moment once confident). **Dive the kite with the front hand** before touchdown; board pointing downwind. | Back hand returning to the bar or out for balance; front hand pulling; board under the body pointing downwind. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Grabbing too early | Rider reaches before getting off the water; legs flick the board up behind; low, unstable jump. | Get airborne first, then knees up and grab. |
| Front hand away from the centre line | Front hand toward the bar end; kite steers off as soon as the back hand releases. | Front hand at the centre line before the jump. |
| Redirecting the kite too early (one-handed) | Kite dives during the grab; rider pulled forward and down. | Keep the kite parked (front hand at the centre); redirect only for the landing. |
| Reaching down instead of bringing the board up | Rider bent over, arm stretched down; legs not tucked. | Knees up to the chest so the board comes to the hand. |
| Leaning back while grabbing | Torso leaning away; rider swinging under the kite. | Upright torso, bar in. |
| No dive for the landing | Lands on an edge with no power, off balance. | Dive firmly with the front hand before touchdown. |

## Drills
- Ride around with the hands next to the centre line, steering push-pull.
- First jumps: just relax the back hand or touch the knee; then touch the board; then grab and hold.
- On the beach: sit and practise reaching the tail with knees tucked.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- Same window as a jump (~3 s before takeoff to ~2 s after landing); densest from the apex onward to see the grab.
- Key checks: front hand position on the bar, timing of the release (before or after leaving the water), knee tuck, board direction at landing.
