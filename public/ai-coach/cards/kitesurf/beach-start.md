# Card: Beach start, twin tip (ביץ' סטארט) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Twin Tip Beach Start). Duotone "HOOKED Beach Start" still to pull (YouTube block).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.beach_start` |
| Level | Beginner → intermediate |
| Prerequisites | Waterstart · one-hand kite control |
| What it is | Starting from shallow water (calf to knee deep), stepping onto the board without sitting in the water. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk in | Front hand holds the board by the front strap; back hand centred on the bar, kite at 11/1 flying away from the beach. | Rider walking in shallow water, board in the front hand, kite forward of 12. |
| 2 | Board drop | Twist upwind and drop the board close to the front foot, pointing upwind. Kite stays forward. | Board on the water next to the front leg, angled upwind; kite still at 11/1. |
| 3 | Front foot in | Kite slowly to 12 with a little bar pulled for support; watch the front foot into the strap. | Rider on one leg, front foot in the strap; lines near vertical. |
| 4 | Kite back | Slowly move the kite to 11 or 1 on the side *away* from where you're going, bar slightly in, toes up to keep the board in place. | Lines leaning back (toward where the rider came from); rider balanced on the back leg in the water. |
| 5 | Steer forward | Steer the kite positively forward across the top of the window; as it rises and lifts, level the bar. | Lines swinging from back through vertical; rider being lifted. |
| 6 | Back foot on | Use the lift (sit in the harness) to step the back foot into the strap without sinking the nose. | Both feet on the board, rider low, board nose up. |
| 7 | Edge and go | Weight onto the heels, twist to look upwind, dive the kite, ride away. | Normal heelside riding position, kite diving forward. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Starting with the kite at 12 | Kite only goes down from 12; nose sinks; rider pulled over the handlebars. | Take the kite back to 11/1 first, then steer forward for lift. |
| Too gentle on the steer | Kite drifts slowly, no lift; rider can't get the back foot on. | Steer forward positively, then level the bar. |
| Board too far away | Rider reaching or stretching for the board; off balance. | Drop the board close to the front foot. |
| Water too deep | Rider above knee deep, struggling to stand. | Calf to knee deep is ideal. |
| Pulling the bar too hard | Kite stalls and bounces. | Bar slightly in for support only. |

## Safety
Room to dive the kite safely (no people, rocks or trees downwind). Soft bottom; avoid very shallow water over hard or rocky ground. Not in dead-onshore wind unless you can walk out far.
