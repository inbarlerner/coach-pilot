# Card: Back roll transition (בק רול טרנזישן) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Back Loop Transition) and Steven Akkersdijk (Jump Transition; 6 Beginner Friendly Tricks).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.hooked.back_roll_transition` |
| Level | Intermediate |
| Prerequisites | Solid [jump transition](jump-transition.md) and [back roll](back-roll.md) |
| What it is | A jump transition with a back roll: the **kite does a jump transition** (park just past 12, redirect to the new side), the **body does a back roll**, and you land going the other way. Slow, controlled rotation. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Slower than for a jump: edge harder to shed speed (not too much, or you stop). Kite slowly to ~11:30. Weight and shoulders toward the back of the board. | Heelside edge, board turning upwind, kite fairly high. |
| 2 | Send + carve | Send the kite to just past 12. Carve **all the way upwind and a bit further**: the extra carve starts the rotation. Front leg nearly straight, back leg coiled. | Board pointing past upwind; big spray; lines vertical. |
| 3 | Pop | The kite is above: explode off the back leg; **the head leads**, looking over the front shoulder as you leave the water. Pull the bar in to the hips, level, so the kite doesn't go further back. | Back leg extended; head turned over the front shoulder; bar in and level; board leaving the water tail first. |
| 4 | Rotate | Front knee drives through toward the wind, then both knees up. Keep the kite around 12 (bar down). | Rider facing upwind, compact; lines vertical. |
| 5 | Spot + dive | At the apex: head finds the landing in the **new** direction; dive the kite firmly toward the new direction. | Head looking down toward the landing; bar tilted hard; lines swinging to the new side. |
| 6 | Land + ride out | Legs down; scissor the legs to point the board downwind (toward the kite); land tail first; carve onto the heel edge and ride away. Untwist only once stable. | Board pointing downwind in the new direction at touchdown; kite diving; spray. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Spinning low without a pop** | Rider spins close to the water on bent legs, landing on the bum. | The head leads, but you still need a full pop off the back leg first. |
| Not carving far enough | Board crosswind at takeoff; rider drifts sideways; no rotation, or no power to land. | Carve past upwind before the pop. |
| Too fast | Rider flies off downwind; no power to land. | Shed speed on the approach by edging. |
| Waiting too long for the kite | Kite flies behind 12 before the pop; lands with no power. | Pop early, as soon as the kite pulls. |
| Kite steered too far past 12 | Lines well past vertical at the apex; dive gives nothing. | Park at 11:30/12:30, bar down. |
| Landing on an edge | Board across the direction of travel at touchdown; abrupt stop. | Scissor the legs so the board points downwind toward the kite. |

## Coaching decisions applied
- **Head always leads** ([coaching-decisions.md](../../coaching-decisions.md)). IKSURFMAG warns that "throwing" with the head alone makes riders spin low. Our cue: **head leads + full pop**, both at takeoff.

## Tip
If landing downwind on the bad side feels awkward, land it to toeside instead (back roll to toeside).

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- From ~3 s before takeoff to ~2 s after landing; densest from the carve to the apex.
- Key checks: board direction at takeoff (past upwind?), pop vs. no pop, head direction, line angle at the apex, board direction at touchdown.
