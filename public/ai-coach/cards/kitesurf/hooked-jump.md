# Card: Hooked jump (קפיצה) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Jumping), Steven Akkersdijk (Perfect jump, "This detail", 3 ways to land, students' mistakes), Get High with Mike (4 posture mistakes, take-off mistake), Duotone Academy (HOOKED Jump).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.hooked.jump` |
| Level | Beginner → lifelong |
| Prerequisites | Comfortable upwind riding both directions · body drag (you'll lose the board) |
| What it is | Take off by carving upwind as the kite passes overhead, float under the kite, and land going downwind. The base of almost every trick. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Medium speed on a crosswind course, kite at ~45° and no lower than 11/1 for first jumps; bar out at the sweet spot; hands about halfway in. Check the space. | Heelside riding, lines ~45° forward, bar away from the body (arms long). |
| 2 | Send + edge ("magic moment") | Steer the kite up toward 12 at a steady pace. As it climbs, **fight the pull**: drop the hips toward the water, carve upwind, push through the back heel, front leg nearly straight. Bar stays **out** while edging. | Lines rising toward vertical; rider low with bum near the water; board carving upwind with **big spray from the tail**; bar still away from the chest. |
| 3 | Pop / takeoff | Just **before** the kite reaches 12 (centre of the kite pointing into the wind): extend off the back leg and pull the bar in at the same moment. Take off about 45° upwind. | Lines near vertical; body extending, shoulders over the hips (not leaning far back); board leaving the water pointing upwind; bar coming in. |
| 4 | In the air | Bar kept pulled in (hands toward the hips) so the kite stays overhead. Park the kite just past 12 (11:30 / 12:30). Knees up, body compact. Spot the landing. | Lines vertical or slightly past; bar in; knees tucked; rider looking at the landing spot, not at the kite. |
| 5 | Redirect | On the way down, about 2 m above the water: steer the kite forward (front hand) through 12 for lift. | Bar tilting with the front hand pulling; lines swinging forward. |
| 6 | Landing | Legs down, board under the body, **board pointing downwind** (direction of travel), tail first, soft knees; dive the kite and carve back to a crosswind course. | Board pointing downwind at touchdown, knees bent, kite moving forward. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix | Source agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Not carving upwind** (most common, per SA) | Board flat or crosswind at takeoff; little spray; jump goes far, not high. | Carve and edge hard upwind as the kite climbs; take off ~45° upwind. | All agree |
| Taking off too late | Kite already past 12 at takeoff (lines leaning back); weak, short jump. | Pop just before the kite reaches 12. | SA, IKSURFMAG |
| Steering the kite too hard / sending from too low | Lines swing fast from low to past 12; rider yanked forward and downwind; little height; hard landing. | Steer slower and shorter; start at ~45°, not on the water. | SA (IKSURFMAG says be positive; see flag below) |
| Pulling the bar in while edging | Bar at the chest during the approach and send. | Bar out while edging; pull in only at the pop. | Mike, SA, IKSURFMAG (jump transition) |
| Shoulders leaning far back while edging | Shoulders well upwind of the hips, bar pulled close by the lean. | Shoulders over the hips, arms extended, hips driving upwind. | Mike |
| Losing the edge before takeoff | Rider stands up or looks up at the kite; board flattens; kite flies behind. | Keep the stance, look upwind/forward, push through the back heel. | IKSURFMAG |
| Trying to jump up instead of waiting | Rider hops off the board before the kite lifts. | Resist the pull and let the kite lift you; pop as it reaches 12. | IKSURFMAG |
| Steering the kite back and forth in the air | Line angle changing a lot between frames at the top; sudden drop. | Park the kite, then one clear redirect for the landing. | SA |
| Landing with the board across the direction of travel | Board perpendicular to the movement at touchdown; board skips; rider falls. | Point the board downwind (where you're moving). | All agree |
| Redirecting the kite too late / not at all | Kite sitting above or behind at touchdown; rider lands on the bum. | Redirect ~2 m above the water. | All agree |

## ⚠️ For Inbar: the sources disagree
1. **Bar during the send:** the older IKSURFMAG beginner article says keep the bar pulled in while sending. Steven, Mike and IKSURFMAG's jump-transition article say keep the bar **out** while edging and pull in at the pop. **Draft follows the majority (bar out, pull at the pop).**
2. **How hard to steer:** IKSURFMAG says send positively and aggressively for first jumps. Steven says harder steering pulls you forward, not up; steer slower and shorter. **Draft follows Steven** (steady send), with "be decisive" for underpowered riders.

## Drills
- Practise the send without jumping: bring the kite up to 12 while edging, look up to check it reaches 12, don't pull the bar. Then jump with the same timing.
- Start small, on a bigger kite in steady moderate wind.
- Film every session; jump-height apps help compare attempts.

## Safety
Clear space downwind (≥50 m) and to the sides; deep water; no jumping near the beach or over shallow water. If you go higher than expected, **stick to the plan**: park, then redirect as normal; no random steering.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- From ~3 s before takeoff to ~2 s after landing; densest around takeoff and touchdown.
- Key moments: the send (line angle), takeoff (board direction, spray, bar position, body line), touchdown (board direction).
- Height can't be judged reliably from video. Don't guess numbers.
