# Card: Toeside riding (טו-סייד) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Fix Your Toeside).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.toeside` |
| Level | Beginner → intermediate |
| Prerequisites | Solid heelside upwind riding |
| What it is | Riding on the toe edge with the back to the kite. The goal is to mirror heelside: same kite, same commitment, other edge. This card covers switching to toeside while riding in one direction. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Comfortable heelside edge, kite no higher than 1/11, back hand centred on the bar, shoulders upwind of the hips. | Heelside position, lines ~45° forward. |
| 2 | Big hand + stiff torso | Front hand off the bar, held up in front on a bent elbow. Torso stiff and stays that way. | One hand raised in front; upper body set. |
| 3 | Relax the grip | Ease the bar slightly out (not dumping power), so the board points a little more upwind. | Board angle a little more upwind; body unchanged. |
| 4 | Weight forward | Hips move toward the front foot while staying on the edge. | Front knee bending, weight over the front foot. |
| 5 | The switch | Compress the front knee, lift the tail and swing the back foot forward, turning the board 180° under a body that doesn't move. Don't pull on the bar. | Nose pressed in, tail lifted and swinging; upper body and raised hand unchanged. |
| 6 | Stand up | Push off the back leg, hips forward; chest and hips face the nose of the board. | Rider standing taller, hips forward, chest open toward the nose. |
| 7 | Edge | Flex the ankles, drive the knees forward, push against the toe edge. "Seagull" arms out. | Board on the toe edge with spray from the toe rail; knees forward; rider's back to the kite; free arm out. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Pulling on the bar | Bar pulled in; kite stalls or drifts downwind; rider pulled up over the board, slowing and sinking. | Keep line tension at the sweet spot; support comes from the edge, not the bar. |
| Bending over | Rider folded at the waist toward the board; bum toward the kite; board turns downwind. | Stiff torso, chest up; hips forward. |
| Hips facing upwind / twisted back | Hips and chest turned toward the kite instead of the nose. | Chest and hips face the nose of the board. |
| Leaning back after drifting downwind | Rider sitting back, board dragging, stopped. | Reset: weight forward, ankles flexed, edge again. |
| Kite too high | Lines near vertical; no edge possible. | Kite at ~45°. |

## Drills
- Find your preferred toeside foot: usually the same foot you'd lead with on ice, a skateboard or a snowboard. Test both.
- Flat water, steady medium wind, with space downwind to experiment.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- The switch takes ~2–3 s, then a few seconds of riding. Spread the frames across it.
