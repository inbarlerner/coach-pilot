| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.waterstart` |
| Level | Beginner |
| Prerequisites | Kite control with one hand · body drag |
| What it is | Getting up on the board from sitting in the water, using a controlled dive of the kite for power. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up | Kite at 12. Sit in the water, back to the wind. Front hand on the bar; free hand puts the board in front, feet into the straps. | Rider sitting in the water, board in front with the tips sideways to the kite, lines vertical. |
| 2 | Compact | Toes pulled up to hold the board; knees bent to the chest, body close to the board; board across the wind, between rider and kite. | Rider tucked small behind the board; board slightly on edge, one tip pointing to where they'll go. |
| 3 | Dive | Both hands on the bar. Steer the kite down into the power zone toward the direction of travel. **Light wind:** first move the kite slightly back, then dive in a steeper curve. **Strong wind:** shallow dive from 12, stop at about 45°. | Lines moving from vertical toward ~45° forward; rider starting to rise out of the water. |
| 4 | Stand up | Let the kite pull you up and onto the board; push through the back leg; front leg extends as you rise. Use as little power as needed. | Rider rising over the board, back leg loaded, front leg straightening, board starting to plane. |
| 5 | Ride away | Level the kite at about 45°, sink weight into the heels, look where you're going. In light wind, pump the kite in gentle up-and-down "S" curves for power. | Rider standing on a heelside edge, kite at ~45°, spray behind the board. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Diving the kite too hard / too far | Lines go nearly horizontal; rider launched over the board face-first or pulled downwind. | Smaller dive; stop around 45° and level the kite. |
| Not enough dive | Rider rises halfway, then sinks back. | Dive more decisively; in light wind, move the kite back first, then dive. |
| Standing up straight | Rider stands tall too early, legs straight, board flattens and turns downwind. | Stay compact, let the kite pull you up, stand on the back leg. |
| Board pointing downwind | Board tip toward the kite; rider shoots downwind and loses power. | Board across the wind, set up toward the direction of travel. |
| Pulling the bar in | Bar at the chest during the dive; kite stalls. | Bar at the sweet spot; steer, don't pull. |
| Front leg locked | Front leg straight and loaded; nose dives. | Weight on the back leg; front leg follows. |

## Drills
- Dry-land dive practice with a trainer kite: steer 12 → 2 → 12 smoothly with both hands.
- In the water: short starts both directions, sitting down after a few metres, until it's automatic.

## Safety
Clear space downwind (the first metres can go downwind). Never wrap the board leash or lines around a hand.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
