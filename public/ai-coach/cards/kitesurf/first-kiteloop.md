# Card: First kite loop (קייט לופ ראשון) — DRAFT

> For coach review (Inbar). Our own words, distilled from IKSURFMAG (C&K: Learning to Loop part 7: Your First Kite Loop; Kite Loop), Steven Akkersdijk (Road to Kiteloops parts 1–2; students' mistakes). Get High with Mike's kite-loop tutorials still to pull (YouTube block).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `kite.twintip.hooked.first_kiteloop` |
| Level | Intermediate |
| Prerequisites | [Downloop transition](downloop-transition.md) both ways · a **vertical** [jump](hooked-jump.md) (straight up, straight down) · landing with the board pointing downwind at speed |
| What it is | During a small jump, the **back hand** loops the kite a full 360° (backward, against the direction of travel). The kite pulls hard, briefly, then catches you. First loops: small, low power, late. |

## Stages, and what each looks like in a frame

| # | Stage | What should happen | What it looks like in a frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Jump approach with a good edge and enough speed; kite at 11/1; hands about a thumb's width out from the centre. Just enough power for a gentle jump. | Heelside edge, lines ~45° forward. |
| 2 | Send for a vertical jump | Steer the kite steadily up the edge of the window (don't yank it across); hold the edge long, heels dug in. | Lines rising to vertical; long spray; board carving upwind. |
| 3 | Take off with the kite at 12 | **Level the bar** so the kite stops just after 12; kick off the edge, **then** pull the bar in. Goal: straight up, kite overhead. | Lines vertical at takeoff; rider going up, not forward. |
| 4 | Wait | Rise with the bar level and in, knees up, looking slightly forward. If the kite is behind 12, a small front-hand tweak brings it to 12. **Don't loop before the apex.** | Lines vertical; rider compact, rising. |
| 5 | Pull the loop | Past the apex, on the way down (first tries: late, close to the water): bar fully in, **back hand pulls hard** (elbow to the side), front hand pushes, ≥45° of bar rotation. **Head turns to look slightly back toward the back hand / where the pull will come from.** | Bar rotated hard (back hand down); lines swinging back; head turned toward the back hand. |
| 6 | Follow the pull | Keep the bar locked in place until the kite has come round and is rising. Tense the stomach, pull the legs in; turn the hips so the board points toward the kite, just behind downwind. | Rider pulled sideways and downwind; legs tucked; board turning toward the kite. |
| 7 | Land + ride out | Land tail first, over the board, pointing downwind. Then carve back onto the heel edge so you don't outrun the kite; ease the bar out and steer the kite forward. | Board pointing downwind at touchdown; then a carve back to heelside. |

## Common mistakes: what you see, and the fix

| Mistake | What you see in frames | Fix | Source agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Looping before the apex** | Loop pulled while still rising; much more power; body stretched out behind; crash. | Wait until you're descending. | All agree |
| Kite behind 12 when looping | Lines leaning back at the pull; slow loop, hard drop, kite slow to recover. | Level the bar at takeoff so the kite stops at 12. | IKSURFMAG, SA |
| Forward jump instead of vertical | Rider travelling across the frame in the air; kite yanked across the window at the send. | Edge longer, steer slower along the edge of the window. | SA |
| Bar not fully in / steering gently | Big, wide loop; strong long pull. | Bar fully in, steer hard: small loop, short pull. | All agree |
| Stopping the pull mid-loop | Bar returns to level while the kite is still diving. | **Keep pulling** until the kite rises again. | IKSURFMAG |
| Looking forward during the loop | Rider twisted off balance by the pull. | Look back toward the pull (back hand) as you loop. | IKSURFMAG |
| Legs left behind | Rider stretched out horizontally, can't get the board under. | Tense the stomach, pull the knees in. | SA |
| Bar held in after landing (stalling) | Kite stays low in front; very fast, unstable landing. | Ease the bar out so the kite can climb. | IKSURFMAG |

## ⚠️ For Inbar: timing for first loops
- **IKSURFMAG:** start **super late**, just before touchdown, then move earlier session by session toward the apex.
- **Steven:** pull at the apex.
- Both agree: never before the apex. **Draft:** first loops late on the way down, then build toward the apex. Inbar to confirm.

## Coaching decisions applied
- **Head always leads** ([coaching-decisions.md](../../coaching-decisions.md)): as the loop is pulled, the head turns toward the pull (back hand), and the hips and board follow the kite for the landing.

## Safety
Only in moderate, steady wind (roughly 15–25 kn) on a kite you control well, never overpowered. Lots of space downwind, deep water. An impact vest is recommended.

## For the AI: filming and frames needed
- From ~3 s before takeoff to ~3 s after landing; densest from the apex to touchdown.
- The kite is usually **out of frame** during the loop: infer it from line direction, bar rotation and the rider's sideways pull, and say so.
- Key checks: line angle at takeoff (12?), timing of the pull relative to the apex, bar fully in, head direction at the pull, board direction at touchdown.

## Progression notes (separate cards)
- Loop earlier toward the apex; kite loop with a small downloop for higher jumps; late back roll kite loop; megaloops.
